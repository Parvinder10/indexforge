FEDERAL ACQUISITION REGULATION (FAR)
PART 12 - ACQUISITION OF COMMERCIAL PRODUCTS AND COMMERCIAL SERVICES
DOCUMENT TYPE: regulation
SOURCE: Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council
DATE: 2026-07-01

Subpart 12.102 - Applicability and Policy
(a) This part prescribes policies and procedures unique to the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services. It implements the Federal Government preference for the acquisition of commercial items established by Title VIII of the Federal Acquisition Streamlining Act of 1994.

(b) Agencies shall:
(1) Conduct market research to determine whether commercial products, commercial services, or non-developmental items are available that meet the agency requirements;
(2) Acquire commercial products or commercial services when they are available to meet the needs of the agency; and
(3) Require prime contractors and subcontractors at all tiers to incorporate, to the maximum extent practicable, commercial products or commercial services as components of items to be supplied to the agency.

Subpart 12.203 - Procedures for Solicitation and Evaluation
Contracts for commercial items must use competitive solicitation notices published on the federal procurement portal. Solicitations shall clearly state the evaluation factors, including technical capability, past performance history, and price reasonableness.
