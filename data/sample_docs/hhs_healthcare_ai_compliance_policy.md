# HHS Policy Directive: Governance and Compliance for Healthcare Artificial Intelligence

**Document Type:** policy  
**Source:** Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)  
**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Directive Number:** HHS-OIG-2026-104  

## 1. Regulatory Context
The Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and the Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology (ONC) establish mandatory governance principles for algorithmic decision support, diagnostic machine learning systems, and generative artificial intelligence integrated within healthcare provider workflows and electronic health record (EHR) platforms.

## 2. Core Compliance Standards
- **Model Explainability and Algorithmic Bias Mitigation:** Healthcare organizations must validate algorithmic training datasets to prevent demographic disparities. Clinical decision algorithms must output confidence metrics and explainable feature attributions for attending clinicians.
- **HIPAA Data Privacy and De-identification:** Training corpora and cloud inference pipelines processing Protected Health Information (PHI) must adhere to Safe Harbor cryptographic de-identification standards or expert statistical determinations under 45 CFR Part 164.
- **Continuous Post-Deployment Audit:** Automated logging must record model versioning, input telemetry, generated clinical summaries, and physician override decisions for a minimum retention window of seven years.
- **Adverse Outcome Reporting:** System anomalies, clinical misclassifications, and software regressions producing unintended patient harm must be reported to HHS and FDA surveillance databases within 48 hours.
