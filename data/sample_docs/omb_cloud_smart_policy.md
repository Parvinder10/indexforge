# OMB Memorandum M-19-19: Federal Cloud Smart Policy
**Source:** Office of Management and Budget (OMB)  
**Date:** 2026-02-15  
**Document Type:** policy  
**Identifier:** DOC-OMB-2026-001  

## 1. Purpose and Overview
The Federal Cloud Smart Strategy provides actionable guidance to executive agencies to modernize their digital infrastructure through thoughtful, secure cloud adoption. Building on the foundation of Cloud First, this policy shifts the focus from simple infrastructure migration to a holistic approach encompassing procurement, cybersecurity, and workforce modernization.

## 2. Key Pillars of Cloud Smart
### 2.1 Security
Agencies must implement modern, zero-trust cybersecurity architectures rather than relying strictly on legacy perimeter-based defenses. All cloud environments hosting federal data must comply with the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP) baseline requirements. Continuous monitoring, automated vulnerability disclosures, and cryptographic access controls are mandatory.

### 2.2 Procurement
Procurement teams must leverage agile acquisition methods. Long-term monolithic contracts are discouraged in favor of modular, performance-based public tenders that allow iterative software enhancements. Vendor lock-in must be minimized by adopting open standards, containerized workloads, and cloud-neutral APIs.

### 2.3 Workforce
Equipping federal employees with high-performance cloud engineering, DevOps, and data governance skills is essential. Agencies should establish digital service academies and technical career ladders to foster in-house software architecture capabilities.

## 3. Implementation and Compliance
Within 180 days of this memorandum, Chief Information Officers (CIOs) must submit updated multi-year cloud migration roadmaps detailing legacy data center consolidations, projected cost savings, and risk management profiles.
