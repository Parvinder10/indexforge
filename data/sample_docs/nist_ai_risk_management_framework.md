# NIST Special Publication 1270: Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

**Document Type:** regulation  
**Source:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)  
**Date:** 2026-03-22  
**Publication:** NIST AI RMF 1.0  

## Executive Summary
This document provides federal guidelines, benchmark methodologies, and operational safeguards to cultivate trustworthy and responsible Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems. As artificial intelligence technologies become integral to national infrastructure, scientific discovery, and automated governance, organizations must proactively manage sociotechnical risks.

## The Core Functions
The AI Risk Management Framework organizes risk mitigation into four continuous, interdependent functions:
1. **Govern:** Cultivate an organizational culture of risk management, clear accountability hierarchies, third-party vendor risk assessments, and ethical compliance review boards.
2. **Map:** Systematically document context, intended use cases, data provenance, potential downstream societal impacts, and interdependencies of algorithmic models.
3. **Measure:** Employ rigorous quantitative testing, adversarial evaluation (red-teaming), bias metrics, uncertainty quantification, and benchmark reproducibility.
4. **Manage:** Allocate resources to identified risks, execute fallback operational procedures, implement human-in-the-loop oversight mechanisms, and conduct post-deployment telemetry monitoring.
