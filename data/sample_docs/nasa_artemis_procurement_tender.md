# Public Tender Solicitation: Autonomous Lunar Navigation and Communications

**Document Type:** tender  
**Source:** National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA)  
**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Solicitation ID:** NASA-ARTEMIS-2026-089  

## 1. Scope of Solicitation
The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) issues this public tender for the procurement, deployment, and operational validation of autonomous surface navigation systems, multi-spectrum optical communications, and lunar gateway telemetry transceivers supporting the Artemis lunar exploration initiative.

## 2. Technical Architecture Requirements
Vendors and contractor consortia submitting proposals must satisfy stringent fault-tolerant criteria:
- **Optical Laser Communication:** High-throughput downlink exceeding 1.2 Gbps optical communications from lunar orbital altitude to ground reception stations.
- **Autonomous SLAM Navigation:** Surface rovers and descent modules must execute real-time Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) without relying on Earth GPS or terrestrial timing beacons.
- **Radiation Hardened Microelectronics:** Processing units must withstand cumulative ionizing radiation doses of 100 krad(Si) with automated single-event latchup (SEL) protection.
- **Cryptographic Security:** End-to-end CNSA 2.0 quantum-resistant encryption algorithms for all telemetry command links.

## 3. Proposal Submission and Evaluation Criteria
Competitive proposals will be scored based on technical architecture feasibility (40%), schedule risk mitigation (30%), past federal procurement performance (20%), and cost efficiency (10%). Proposals must be uploaded through the federal acquisition portal no later than September 30, 2026.
