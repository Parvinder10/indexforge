# Technical Documentation: Renewable Energy Grid Integration and Emission Standards
**Agency:** Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Office of Air and Radiation  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Document Type:** regulation  
**Docket:** EPA-HQ-OAR-2026-0421  

## 1. Statutory Authority and Objective
Under Section 111 of the Clean Air Act, the Environmental Protection Agency establishes greenhouse gas emission standards and regulatory compliance schedules for fossil fuel-fired electric generating units. This document delineates best system of emission reduction (BSER) criteria, carbon capture and storage (CCS) technical readiness milestones, and grid reliability coordination requirements.

## 2. Technical Compliance Criteria
### 2.1 Carbon Capture and Sequestration (CCS)
Base load power generation facilities must attain 90% carbon capture efficiency utilizing validated post-combustion chemical absorption or oxy-combustion technologies by calendar year 2032. Continuous emissions monitoring systems (CEMS) must transmit hourly telemetry to EPA regional offices.

### 2.2 Clean Hydrogen Co-Firing
Intermediate load combustion turbines may comply via co-firing 30% by volume clean low-greenhouse-gas hydrogen by 2032, increasing to 96% hydrogen by volume by 2038. All hydrogen fuel supplies must carry third-party lifecycle emissions certifications.

## 3. Public Review and Tender Solicitations
State environmental agencies must submit implementation plans within 24 months. Municipal power authorities may publish public tender solicitations for engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) contracts to upgrade transmission line interconnects.
