SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION (SEC)
PUBLIC REGULATORY NOTICE & COMPLIANCE BULLETIN
Notice No: SEC-2026-REG-7701
Date: 2026-09-18
Subject: Automated Market Making, Ultra Low-Latency Algorithmic Execution, and Systemic Risk Controls

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY
The Securities and Exchange Commission hereby adopts amended rules under Regulation SCI and the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 governing proprietary algorithmic trading firms, high-frequency liquidity providers, and alternative trading systems (ATS).

2. MANDATED RISK CONTROLS AND ARCHITECTURAL THRESHOLDS
Registered broker-dealers and electronic exchange participants utilizing automated trading algorithms must implement pre-trade risk controls:
- Real-Time Price Banding and Order Size Limits: Hard system barriers preventing errant market order dispatch, runaway algorithm execution, and accidental cascade events.
- Latency Floor and Fair Order Queue Processing: Exchange matching engines must enforce deterministic message processing queues to prevent predatory colocation advantages.
- Automated Kill Switches: Independent, out-of-band circuit breakers capable of immediate algorithm termination within 5 milliseconds of risk parameter breaches.
- Comprehensive Audit Trail Logging: Complete microsecond-level synchronization of all order submissions, cancellations, fills, and internal state transitions with PTP time-stamping (IEEE 1588).

3. NON-COMPLIANCE SANCTIONS
Failure to maintain functional circuit breakers or maintain audit logs will subject market participants to immediate suspension of trading privileges and administrative civil monetary penalties.
